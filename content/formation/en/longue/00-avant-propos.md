# Foreword

This course is not here to sell you the promise of fast, effortless results — that kind of promise, everywhere in the dropshipping world, largely explains why so many sellers give up after a few months of disillusionment. Its goal is to give you a complete method, honest about its limits, and directly applicable to your own business, whether it's already running or not started yet.

Every part of this course was built around one simple principle: explain not only what to do, but why it works, with concrete worked examples rather than vague claims. When a figure quoted in this course doesn't rest on a primary source that can be verified with certainty, it is explicitly presented as an approximation or a figure widely repeated in the industry rather than as an established fact — we believe this honesty about uncertainty is more useful than a false precision that would collapse at the first serious look. Part 21 of this course lists all the sources that informed its writing.

Throughout the parts, you'll find direct references to Dropvio features whenever they concretely answer a problem covered in the text. These mentions are not advertising inserted out of context: they are tools built precisely to apply the method in these pages more easily, and with less risk of error.

This course exists in two versions. The one you're reading covers each topic in depth, with multiple worked examples, case studies, detailed evaluation grids and a full anatomy of the most common failure patterns. A short version, condensed but just as serious, exists for those who want the essential landmarks first before going deeper on specific topics in this long version.

Enjoy the read, and above all enjoy putting it into practice — that's where everything really happens.
