# Part 16 — The toolbox: where each Dropvio feature fits into the method

This course has mentioned, part after part, several Dropvio features at the moment they directly addressed the topic at hand. This part brings them together in one place, to clarify at what exact moment of your process each one comes in.

## When searching for and validating a product

This is the stage where Dropvio's Finder comes in: it queries the AliExpress catalog through its official API and keeps a daily history of order volume to estimate a product's trend — before you compare several potential suppliers of the same product on reliability criteria, the product's average rating and the volume of orders already placed, rather than committing to the first result you come across, as detailed in Part 2 and its supplement. This comparison must always come before, never after, the financial commitment to a given supplier.

## When calculating a margin before launching a campaign

The margin calculator built into Dropvio follows exactly the logic worked through by hand in Part 3 of this course — product cost, transaction fees, import duties and taxes, acquisition cost, return rate, contributions — in the mode for the country you sell to: France, United Kingdom, United States, Belgium, Switzerland, Canada/Quebec, Luxembourg, Germany, Italy, Spain, or a manual international mode for other destinations, so you don't have to redo this calculation by hand for each product and to reduce the risk of forgetting a cost item. Using this tool systematically before every launch, rather than occasionally, is what turns this discipline into a reflex rather than a good intention forgotten under launch pressure.

## When building or checking a product page

The landing page auditor automatically scores the page out of a hundred points across nine criteria — visual hierarchy, white space, typography, product image, call to action, social proof, copy, urgency and price, mobile version — following a logic close to the grid detailed in Part 4 and its supplement, before paid ad traffic is sent to that page. This automated check compensates for the fact that a rigorous manual check, although recommended, often ends up being neglected under the pressure of the launch schedule.

## When building the store itself

The site builder generates a product page or a multi-product store from styles and color palettes, with a mobile preview before publishing, as recommended in Part 4. The result can be exported to Shopify; with a connected Shopify store, products are sent there and kept up to date, and orders and tracking numbers stay in sync. Other tools are planned but not yet available — a Chrome extension for importing from AliExpress, Alibaba as a second product source, AI image generation and a catalog of bulky products: until they're released, don't build your method around them.

## Once the business is running, to track after-sales incidents

For stores connected through Shopify, centralize the tracking of refunds and disputes yourself rather than manually rebuilding that view from scattered emails — with the honest distinction between a real absence of disputes and data that's simply not available depending on the payment method used, a point of rigor that keeps you from drawing falsely reassuring conclusions from a zero figure that would in reality mean nothing.

## To step back and look at the whole business

Beyond each tool taken individually, Dropvio's main dashboard brings together the indicators mentioned in Part 8 as the real steering benchmarks — not just gross revenue, but the elements that let you build a more honest picture of the real health of the business.

## A useful reminder to close this part

None of these tools replaces the method itself detailed in the previous parts — a margin calculator is useless if you ignore its result because launch enthusiasm pushes you to go ahead anyway, and an incident dashboard is useless if no one checks it regularly. These tools reduce the workload and the risk of execution errors in an already solid method; they never replace the discipline of applying it.
